The lesson

A base-case DCF on this gold project returned a negative NPV — on a single-point basis, a rejection. Run through Monte Carlo, the same project's mean NPV was positive, with a 69.2% probability of profit. A base case is one combination of point estimates, not the most likely outcome and not automatically the conservative one. It can make a genuinely good project look like a reject just as easily as it can overstate a bad one.
